# Demo 1 — Lead-Qualification Agent

**Label: Demo — fictional data. This is not client work.**

A fictional home-services company (Northwind Home Services, fictional) receives form and email leads. This agent scores each lead against a written rubric, writes qualified leads to a CRM mock, alerts the team, and puts uncertain leads in a human queue with a reason. No lead is lost, and no low-confidence lead is auto-booked.

## How to run

```bash
cd demo1-lead-qualification
python3 lead_agent.py
python3 -m unittest test_lead_agent.py -v
```

Outputs:
- `outputs/decision_log.csv` — every lead, score breakdown, route, destination, reason
- `outputs/summary.json` — counts by route and lead IDs per route

## Pipeline

Intake (mock form/email CSV) → score against rubric → validate → route → log

Rubric (0–100): Budget 30, Timeline 25, Service match 25, Intent 20.
Routing: 70+ Qualified, 40–69 Human queue, below 40 Archive.
Disqualifiers cap the score at 39 and force Archive, except a duplicate, which forces Human queue so it is never double-counted as a new Qualified lead.

In a production version, extraction of structured fields from free-text messages would be done by an LLM. In this demo the structured fields are supplied in the CSV so scoring is fully reproducible and testable; the rubric, validation, routing, and logging are the parts being demonstrated. Do not list LLM extraction as built experience from this demo alone until an LLM extraction step is actually added and tested.

## Walkthrough — three leads to show a buyer

- **L001 — Qualified (100):** High budget, this week, exact service, ready to book. → CRM mock + alert.
- **L009 — Human queue (55):** Medium budget, next 2 months, related service, general question. → Human queue, uncertain intent/timeline named in the reason.
- **L016 — Archive (capped at 39):** Strong on paper, but out of area. → Archive with reason `out_of_area`, still logged, not silently dropped.

## Acceptance criteria (checked by the tests)

- All 20 leads logged, in input order
- Distribution: 8 Qualified, 7 Human queue, 5 Archive
- Every score breakdown sums to its raw score
- No Qualified lead is below 70, a duplicate, out of area, a service not offered, below minimum budget, or a spam probe
- Every Human-queue item names a reason
- Duplicate L015 (same email as L001) is Human queue, not a second Qualified lead
